How this optimizer works

Transparent algorithm, no black-box promises.

1. Critical Path Method

Topological sorting, forward pass (ES/EF), backward pass (LS/LF), and float calculation. This gives the precedence-only lower bound.

2. Capacity-constrained scheduling

A serial schedule generation scheme places eligible tasks at the earliest date where their team has available units, while respecting dependencies.

3. Multi-start optimization

Multiple priority rules and seeded randomized task orders are tested. The shortest valid feasible result is retained; global optimality is not guaranteed.

4. Cost accounting

Fixed task charges and assigned daily team rates are summed. With fixed allocations, shifting dates changes project duration, not cost.

This is a planning and educational tool, not certified project-management or engineering software. It assumes whole-day tasks, finish-to-start dependencies, fixed resource needs and no holidays beyond weekends.
